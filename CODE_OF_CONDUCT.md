# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about code, not
about people.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project better:

- Giving and gracefully accepting specific, technical feedback
- Saying what you actually ran and what it printed, rather than what you
  believe to be true
- Acknowledging when you were wrong, briefly, and moving on
- Focusing on what is best for people using the framework

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and the project's public accounts — and when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **78920650+zvndev@users.noreply.github.com**.
Reports are handled privately and promptly. Whoever reports something will not
be identified without their agreement.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain
the reason when doing so is appropriate.
